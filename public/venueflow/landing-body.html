Guest park Memberships

Season and recurring memberships for Growth+

Entitled Growth and Enterprise companies configure membership plans, multi-venue eligibility, and guest CRM profiles in company admin — and sell, renew, upgrade/downgrade, pause, cancel, and recover failed payments from Memberships → Members (Stripe when configured; collected/pending honesty without it). Guests manage linked memberships online (Profile → Memberships: upgrade/renew/cancel/card, click-to-cancel, agreements). Staff get real sold/active/churn reports and Enterprise advanced dunning. Redeem/verify at the Redeem desk enforces CURRENT / VISITING / INELIGIBLE with family shared visits (beta). Stable POS/online verify adapters are live; full POS till UI ships later. Starter packages stay on ticketing; Demo keeps Memberships locked.
